Privacy Policy

Universal Combo (mobile screen guard compatible model finder)

Short summary (Hindi): Universal Combo aapka sirf zaroori data leta hai: login ke liye email, app ko behtar banane ke liye search/usage ginti, feedback, aur notification ke liye device token. Hum aapka data bechte nahi hain, aur koi ads nahi dikhate. Aap kabhi bhi email karke apna account aur data delete karwa sakte hain.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Universal Combo app and website ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is built and operated by Mahendra Kumar, India. The App is a free tool for mobile accessories dealers to find compatible tempered glass / screen guard models.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Email address and account ID (when you create an account or sign in with email or Google)To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
Name shown on your Google account (only if you sign in with Google)To show your name in the App and with feedback you send.
Join date, last active time, and basic activity events (such as signup and app open)To keep the App working and understand how many people use it.
Search queries and usage counts (for example which phone brands or models are searched, and how many times the App is shared)To improve the model database and show the most useful results.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, or microphone data. If you buy a premium plan, your card, UPI, or bank details are entered on the payment provider's secure page and are never stored by us. Optional shop stock lists you create in the App are kept on your own device.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, secure, and improve the App.
  • To let you sign in and keep your account working.
  • To review feedback and update requests and keep the glass compatibility data accurate.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show advertisements, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. Services we use

The App uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, and Cloud Messaging) to store account data and send notifications. Firebase is operated by Google and processes data on our behalf. If you buy a premium plan, payments are processed by Razorpay (razorpay.com/privacy). Product photos of spare parts added by the App administrator are hosted on Cloudinary; users do not upload photos. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. The App also loads fonts and code libraries from Google and Cloudflare content networks. We do not share your information with any other third party, except when required by law.

4. Data stored on your device

The App stores small amounts of data in your browser or device (local storage, session storage, and the service worker cache) to remember your settings, speed up loading, and let parts of the App work with a weak connection. You can clear this at any time from your device or browser settings.

5. Data sharing

Your data is visible only to the App owner/administrator and to the service providers described above. Your email is never shown to other users.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. Feedback and usage records are kept as long as they are useful for improving the App. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it as described in section 8.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with Google Firebase. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Account and data deletion

How to delete your account and data:

  • In the App: open the menu and tap Delete My Account (you must be signed in).
  • Or send an email to M.k.p.tyoup@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Universal Combo account", from the email address you used to sign in.
  • We will delete your account, email address, activity records, and notification token within 30 days (usually much sooner) and confirm by email.
  • Feedback you sent may be deleted on request, or kept in a form that no longer identifies you.

Account delete karne ke liye app ke menu me "Delete My Account" dabayein, ya upar diye email par apne login wale email se likh kar bhejein. Aapka account aur data 30 din ke andar hata diya jayega.

9. Children

The App is meant for mobile accessories dealers and general users and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
